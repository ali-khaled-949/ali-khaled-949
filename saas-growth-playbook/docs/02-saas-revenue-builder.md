# 2. SaaS Revenue Builder: path to $5,000 MRR

## Pricing tiers that make $5K realistic
Target mix at $5K MRR (blended ARPU is about $50):

| Plan | Price | Customers | MRR |
|------|-------|-----------|-----|
| Solo | $29 | 55 | $1,595 |
| Pro | $59 | 40 | $2,360 |
| Studio | $149 | 7 | $1,043 |
| **Total** | | **~102** | **~$5,000** |

About 100 paying customers is the whole target, which is achievable for a niche B2B tool.

Levers: the Pro plan is the default, highlighted as "Most popular". Studio anchors the price. A usage wall (5 projects) naturally upgrades successful freelancers.

## Features that prevent cancellation after month 1
- **Accumulated data:** templates, past proposals, and estimate calibration. Leaving means losing them.
- **Live Scope Watch** on active projects. Cancelling in the middle of a project feels risky.
- **Monthly "Money Recovered" report** emailed on the 1st, before the renewal charge.
- **Client-facing links** (proposals, change orders) stay live only on paid plans.
- **Pause plan ($5/mo)** instead of cancel, for freelancers between contracts.

## Low-cost acquisition for the ideal customer
1. **Free tools as SEO and lead magnets:** "Freelance project estimate calculator", "Change order template generator", "Scope-of-work template (dev)". Each one has a single CTA to ScopeGuard.
2. **Build in public** on X and LinkedIn: weekly MRR plus one lesson. Developers follow developers.
3. **Community presence:** answer scope-creep threads on Reddit and Indie Hackers with real help and a link only when relevant.
4. **Partnerships:** freelance coaches, bootcamps, and Upwork/Toptal newsletters. Offer 30% recurring affiliate commission.
5. **Referral loop:** see doc 03.
Target CAC is under $40, against an LTV of about $50 × 14 months ≈ $700.

## Realistic MRR projection
Assumptions: 5% monthly churn by month 3 (dropping to 3.5%), 3–4% visitor→trial, 20% trial→paid, and traffic growing through SEO.

| Month | New paid/mo | Customers | MRR |
|-------|-------------|-----------|-----|
| 3 | ~15 | ~35 | **~$1,600** |
| 6 | ~20 | ~65 | **~$3,200** |
| 12 | ~25 | ~110 | **~$5,500** |

$5K MRR at about month 10–12 is the realistic case. Month 6 is possible only if one channel (SEO or a partnership) takes off.
