# 4. Launch Expert: the first 7 days

## Waitlist strategy (4–6 weeks before launch)
- **Landing page:** hero line + a 40-second demo GIF (notes → proposal) + a "Scope-creep cost calculator" that shows how much they lost last year. Asking for an email to unlock the result converts well.
- **Referral-ranked waitlist:** "Move up the list: each invite = 10 spots. Top 100 get founding pricing." (Use Viral Loops or a simple custom counter.)
- **Weekly build-in-public email:** a screenshot, one lesson, and one question ("What's your worst scope-creep story?"). Replies become testimonials and content.
- **Beta cohort:** let in 20 people 2 weeks early for bugs and testimonials.
- Target: **500–1,000 waitlist emails** from the calculator, Reddit/IH posts, and your personal network.

## Exact launch-day sequence (Tuesday or Wednesday)
| Time (PT) | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| 00:01 | Product Hunt goes live, with a founder comment telling the personal story |
| 06:00 | Email 1 to the waitlist: "You're in. Founding price ends Friday midnight." |
| 07:00 | X/LinkedIn launch thread with a demo video, then DM the top 50 supporters personally |
| 08:00 | Show HN: "I built a tool that catches scope creep in client emails" |
| 09:00–12:00 | Posts in r/freelance, r/webdev, IH, and Slack/Discord communities (value-first, tailored for each) |
| All day | Reply to every comment within 10 minutes and onboard sign-ups personally |
| 18:00 | Email 2 to non-openers with a different subject line |
| Day 2–3 | Publish beta user case studies ("How Sam recovered $2,100") |
| Day 5 | Email: "48 hours left on founding pricing" + a count of seats remaining |
| Day 7 (Fri 23:59) | Founding pricing closes. Send a final email at 18:00 |

## Early-user incentive that creates urgency
**Founding Member:** Pro at **$19/mo for life** (normally $59), or **$149 lifetime for year 1 + 50% off forever**. It is capped at **100 seats**, shows a live seat counter, and **closes after 7 days**. This is real scarcity, so honour it and don't extend it.

## Realistic first-week revenue estimate
From an interested audience of about 800 waitlist emails plus launch traffic:
- Waitlist: 800 × 45% open × 12% click-to-trial ≈ 43 trials
- Launch traffic (PH + HN + Reddit): about 3,000 visitors × 3% ≈ 90 trials
- About 130 trials × 25% founding conversion (urgency lifts it) ≈ **30–35 paid**
- Mix of monthly ($19) and annual prepay (~30% choose $190/yr):
  - **Cash collected in week 1: roughly $1,500–$2,500**
  - **MRR added: roughly $600–$750**

Downside case (weak PH day): 10–15 paid, about $400 cash. Upside (HN front page): 60+ paid.
