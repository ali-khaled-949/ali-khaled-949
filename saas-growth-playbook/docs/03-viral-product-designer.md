# 3. Viral Product Designer: growth without paid ads

## "Hidden gem" value proposition
> **"The tool that pays for itself the first time a client says 'can you just…'."**

It feels like an insider secret because it solves a pain freelancers complain about constantly but assume can't be fixed. It is also opinionated for developers (it understands "add OAuth" ≠ "change a button colour"), unlike generic proposal tools such as PandaDoc or Bonsai.

Supporting details: a personal "scope-creep saved" counter, a founder-written changelog, and a small invite-only community channel.

## Ideal moment inside the product to ask for sharing
**Right after the first accepted change order.** The user just earned money they would otherwise have lost. Show:
> "🎉 You just recovered **$840**. Know a freelancer who needs this? Give them 1 month of Pro free and you get 1 month free."

Secondary moments: when a client e-signs a proposal, and in the monthly "Money Recovered" report, which has a shareable card for X and LinkedIn.

## Built-in distribution (the product markets itself)
Every proposal and change order the client receives has a subtle footer: *"Scoped with ScopeGuard."* Clients are often agencies or founders who hire other freelancers, so each document shown to a client is a free impression. Paid plans can remove the footer, but most won't bother.

## Network effect that grows with users
- **Rate and estimate benchmarks:** anonymized, opt-in data answers questions like "A Stripe integration typically takes 12–18h; you estimated 6h." This gets more accurate as more users join, and a solo tool can't copy it.
- **Template marketplace:** users publish proposal and SOW templates by stack or niche (Shopify, React Native, …), and popular authors get credit and referrals.
- **Subcontracting (later):** agencies on Studio can send out scoped work packages to other ScopeGuard freelancers.

## Realistic organic growth estimate
- Referral: about 10–15% of paying users refer at least one person, and about 30% of those convert. That gives a K-factor of **~0.05–0.1**, which helps but is not true virality.
- Footer impressions: about 3–5 client views per active user per month, with roughly 0.3% signing up.
- Combined with SEO and community, expect **8–15% month-over-month organic growth** in months 3–12. Be skeptical of anyone promising K > 1 for B2B tools.
